# 🌟 Eevee's Silly Evolution Disco & Arcade 🌟

An interactive 3D Three.js experience dedicated to Pokémon's most versatile, lovable creature: **Eevee**, and all 8 of its legendary "Eeveelutions"!

---

## 🎮 How to Play & Explore

### 1. Opening the Experience
- **Direct in Browser**: You can double-click `index.html` to open it in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.
- **Local Dev Server**: A local web server is currently running at:
  👉 **`http://localhost:8099/`**

---

## ✨ Features & Game Modes

### 🧸 Mode 1: The Petting & Toybox Playground
- **Interactive Petting**: Click or touch Eevee directly on screen to give gentle chin or ear scratches! Eevee squishes down with spring physics, purrs happily, wags its fluffy tail, and gets glowing pink heart eyes `( ^ ‿ ^ )`!
- **Treat Slingshot**:
  - 🍓 **Feed Oran Berry**: Launches a 3D berry that Eevee tracks in mid-air and snaps up with a comical crunch!
  - 🧁 **Poke-Puff (Chonky)**: Triggers **Gigantamax Floof Mode**—Eevee puffs up like a giant round fluffy cloud!
- **🪩 Disco Fever**: Lowers a sparkling 3D disco ball with rotating multi-colored spotlights, kickstarting a groovy chiptune dance party where Eevee shows off silly dance steps!
- **🍞 Bread Loaf Mode**: Paws are tucked away in a 10/10 golden loaf!
- **🚨 The Roomba Alarm**: Unleash Eevee's arch-nemesis—a runaway robotic vacuum cleaner with glowing red headlights! Eevee gets spiral panic eyes and dashes around in chaotic circles!
- **📸 Goofy Photo Mode**: Freezes the action, triggers a screen flash, and automatically downloads a high-resolution PNG snapshot (`eevee_goofy_moment.png`).

---

### 🎮 Mode 2: "The Great Stone Dash & Munch" (Active Arcade Runner)
- **Gameplay**: Toggle to **Stone Dash** mode to launch an active 3D runner game on an endless floating rainbow conveyor!
- **Controls**:
  - **Steering**: `A` / `D` or `Left` / `Right` arrow keys, or simply drag your mouse / touch across the screen!
  - **Jump**: `Space`, `W`, or `Up` arrow key!
- **Mechanics**:
  - Catch falling **Evolution Stones** (Water, Thunder, Fire, Leaf, Ice, Sun, Moon, Fairy Ribbon).
  - Collecting a stone triggers an **instant mid-run anime transformation** into that Eeveelution with super-abilities (e.g. Jolteon Thunder Speed, Vaporeon Hydro Shield, Flareon Flame Blast)!
  - Dodge goofy hazards: slippery banana peels (causing 360 cartoon wipeouts) and rolling Voltorbs!
  - Rack up high scores, combo streaks, and keep your 3 hearts intact!

---

### 🧬 Mode 3: The Eeveelution Lore & Fun Facts Encyclopedia
- Interactive bottom selector dock supporting all 9 forms:
  1. 🦊 **Eevee** (Normal)
  2. 🧜‍♂️ **Vaporeon** (Water)
  3. ⚡ **Jolteon** (Electric)
  4. 🔥 **Flareon** (Fire)
  5. 🔮 **Espeon** (Psychic)
  6. 🌙 **Umbreon** (Dark)
  7. 🍃 **Leafeon** (Grass)
  8. ❄️ **Glaceon** (Ice)
  9. 🎀 **Sylveon** (Fairy)
- **3D Procedural Morphing**: Each form features unique 3D silhouettes, colors, and attachments (aquatic fins, spiky needle quills, psychic forehead gem, ice bangs, leaf sprout, or flowing fairy ribbons).
- **✨ Shiny Mode**: One-click toggle transforms any form into its rare Shiny color palette!
- **🎲 Random Fact Shuffler**: Pulls authentic Pokémon biology lore and hilarious community memes into animated pop-up speech bubbles.

---

### 🎵 100% Web Audio Synthesizer
- Zero external MP3/WAV dependencies!
- Catchy upbeat 8-bit disco chiptune with walking bass, melody arpeggios, kick, snare, and hi-hats.
- Real-time procedural sound effects: boing leaps, munch crunches, zaps, water splashes, purrs, and evolution arpeggio fanfares.
