## Processing Log & Pipeline
- **Asset Processing Engine**: Blender 4.5.7 LTS (Headless CLI)
- **Pipeline Script**: `tools/export_all_species.py`
- **Runtime Target**: Three.js WebGL with `THREE.GLTFLoader`
- **Transform Normalization**:
  - Paws rested at ground level `y = 0.0`.
  - Forward direction dynamically aligned along `+Z` (towards camera) using spine heading vector `Math.atan2(head.x - waist.x, head.z - waist.z)`.
  - Species height normalized: Eevee ~0.95m, adult evolutions ~1.35m.
  - Decoupled simplified invisible cylinder colliders (`THREE.CylinderGeometry(0.55, 0.55, 1.3)`) for arcade runner and petting click raycasting.
